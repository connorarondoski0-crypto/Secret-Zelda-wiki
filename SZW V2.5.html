A Link to the Past (ALttP)

Info and lore from A Link to the Past (1991):

The Light World & The Dark World

Set in the Downfall Timeline, Hyrule is threatened by the dark wizard Agahnim, who breaks the ancient seal of the Seven Wise Men. Link must travel between the peaceful Light World and the corrupted Dark World—the twisted reflection of the Sacred Realm shaped by Ganon's evil wish.

The Three Pendants & The Master Sword

Guided by the telepathic cries of Princess Zelda and the elder Sahasrahla, Link gathers the Pendants of Courage, Power, and Wisdom to pull the legendary Master Sword from its pedestal deep within the Lost Woods.

The Seven Maidens & Ganon

Agahnim kidnaps seven maidens—descendants of the Seven Sages, including Zelda—to banish them to the Dark World. Link rescues each maiden from crystal prisons across treacherous dungeons before confronting Ganon inside the Pyramid of Power to reclaim the complete Triforce.

Ocarina of Time (OoT)

Info and lore from Ocarina of Time (1998):

Link & Navi

Also known as the Hero of Time. Raised as a Kokiri child without a fairy until Navi is sent by the Great Deku Tree, he gathers the three Spiritual Stones and pulls the Master Sword from the Temple of Time, sealing his spirit for 7 years until he is old enough to wield the blade.

Zelda & Sheik

Princess of Hyrule who foresees Ganondorf's betrayal in her prophetic dreams. During Ganon's 7-year reign of terror, she survives in hiding disguised as the Sheikah warrior Sheik, teaching Link warp melodies and bearing the Triforce of Wisdom.

Ganondorf

The sole male born to the Gerudo tribe every 100 years. He tricks Link and Zelda into opening the Door of Time, enters the Sacred Realm, and touches the Triforce—causing it to split into three pieces while leaving him with the Triforce of Power.

The Ocarina & Seven Sages

Using the royal Ocarina of Time, Link manipulates time and awakens the Seven Sages—Rauru, Saria, Darunia, Ruto, Impa, Nabooru, and Zelda—who combine their power to seal Ganon inside the Evil Realm.

Majora's Mask (MM)

Info and lore from Majora's Mask (2000):

Setting & The Three-Day Cycle

A direct sequel to Ocarina of Time set in the parallel world of Termina while Link searches for his lost friend Navi. Link has only 72 hours before a leering moon crashes into Clock Town, forcing him to repeatedly play the Song of Time to rewind the three-day cycle.

Skull Kid & Majora's Mask

A lonely Skull Kid steals a cursed, ancient hexing mask from the Happy Mask Salesman. The dark entity trapped inside Majora's Mask possesses Skull Kid, exploiting his grief over being abandoned by his friends to pull the moon from its orbit.

Transformation Masks & The Fierce Deity

By healing sorrowful souls with the Song of Healing, Link obtains masks that transform him into a Deku Scrub, Goron, and Zora to free the Four Giants of Termina. Collecting every mask unlocks the mysterious Fierce Deity's Mask, granting Link godlike power in the final battle.

The Wind Waker (TLOZWW)

Info and lore from The Wind Waker (2002):

Setting: The Great Sea

Set centuries after Ocarina of Time in the Adult Timeline. When Ganondorf broke free from his seal and no Hero appeared, the people prayed to the Golden Goddesses, who flooded Hyrule and sealed the kingdom beneath a vast ocean known as the Great Sea.

Link & Tetra

Known as the Hero of Winds, this Link sets sail from Outset Island to rescue his kidnapped sister Aryll. Unlike past heroes, he has no bloodline connection to the Hero of Time and must earn the Triforce of Courage through his own trials alongside Tetra, a fiery pirate captain who is secretly Princess Zelda.

The King of Red Lions

Link sails across the Great Sea aboard a talking red boat named the King of Red Lions, who is actually the spirit of Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule, the final king of sunken Hyrule.

Key Artifact

Link wields the Wind Waker, a sacred conductor's baton once used by the Hyrulean Royal Family to conduct prayers to the gods and command the winds.

Twilight Princess (TLOZTP)

Info and lore from Twilight Princess (2006):

Setting & Wolf Transformation

Set in the Child Timeline roughly a century after Ocarina of Time. Hyrule is gradually swallowed by curtains of Twilight from a parallel dimension; when Link enters the Twilight, the Triforce of Courage protects him by transforming him into a divine wolf rather than a wandering spirit.

Midna & The Twili

An impish shadow being from the Twilight Realm who rides on Wolf Link's back. She is revealed to be the true Twilight Princess, ruler of the Twili—descendants of ancient dark interlopers banished to the Twilight Realm by the Goddesses.

Zant, Ganondorf & The Mirror of Twilight

Usurper King Zant overthrows Midna and invades Hyrule using dark magic granted by Ganondorf, who survived his failed execution at the Arbiter's Grounds. Zant shatters the Mirror of Twilight into four Fused Shadow shards to sever the connection between worlds.

Skyward Sword (TLOZSS)

Info and lore from Skyward Sword (2011):

Timeline Origins

The very first chapter in the official Zelda timeline, revealing the creation of the Master Sword, the mortal incarnation of the Goddess Hylia as Zelda, and the founding of Hyrule on the Surface below.

Skyloft & Loftwings

To protect humanity from the Demon King Demise, the Goddess Hylia lifted the surviving humans and the Triforce into the heavens on floating islands called Skyloft, where each citizen bonds with a guardian bird called a Loftwing.

Fi, Ghirahim & Demise's Curse

Link is guided by Fi, an analytical spirit created by Hylia to reside inside the Goddess Sword, which Link tempers in three Sacred Flames to forge the Master Sword. After defeating Demon Lord Ghirahim and Demise, Demise casts an eternal curse binding his hatred to reincarnate alongside the blood of the Goddess and the spirit of the Hero forever.

Breath of the Wild (BOTW)

Info and lore from Breath of the Wild (2017):

The Great Calamity

10,000 years after ancient Sheikah technology first sealed Calamity Ganon, Ganon returns and corrupts the automated Guardians and four Divine Beasts, mortally wounding Link and destroying Hyrule Castle.

The Slumber of Restoration

Link awakens 100 years later inside the Shrine of Resurrection with amnesia. Guided by the Sheikah Slate and the spirit of King Rhoam, he recovers his lost memories, reclaims the Master Sword from Korok Forest, and rescues Zelda, who spent a full century holding Ganon back inside Hyrule Castle.

The Four Champions

Mipha (Zora), Daruk (Goron), Revali (Rito), and Urbosa (Gerudo) were chosen pilots of the Divine Beasts who fell during the Calamity. Freed by Link, their spirits aim the Divine Beasts at Hyrule Castle and grant Link sacred Champion abilities.

Tears of the Kingdom (TotK)

Info and lore from Tears of the Kingdom (2023):

The Upheaval & The Three Layers of Hyrule

While investigating toxic gloom beneath Hyrule Castle, Link and Zelda awaken a mummified Demon King Ganondorf, triggering the Upheaval. Ancient Sky Islands appear above Hyrule, the Master Sword shatters, Link loses his right arm and receives a replacement Zonai arm from King Rauru, and vast chasms tear open into the subterranean Depths.

The Zonai & The Imprisoning War

Long ago, the godlike Zonai descended from the heavens, and King Rauru married Hyrule's first queen, Sonia. When Ganondorf murdered Sonia and stole her Secret Stone to become the Demon King, Rauru and the ancient Sages fought him in the Imprisoning War, with Rauru sacrificing himself to seal Ganondorf beneath Hyrule Castle.

Zelda's Sacrifice & The Light Dragon

Sent back in time to the founding era of Hyrule by her Secret Stone, Zelda realizes the shattered Master Sword can only be restored by bathing in sacred power for millennia. She swallows her Secret Stone—performing forbidden "draconification" to become the immortal Light Dragon—carrying the healed Master Sword in her forehead across thousands of years until Link retrieves it in the skies above Hyrule.

The New Sages & Final Battle

With his Zonai arm abilities—Ultrahand, Fuse, Ascend, and Recall—Link awakens a new generation of Sages: Tulin (Wind), Yunobo (Fire), Sidon (Water), Riju (Lightning), and Mineru (Spirit). Together they descend into the Depths beneath Hyrule Castle to defeat Demon King Ganondorf and his Demon Dragon form, restoring Zelda back to her human self.

Deep Lore & Timeline Secrets

Core mythology, hidden connections, and secrets across the Legend of Zelda universe:

The Three Golden Goddesses & The Triforce

Before time began, three Golden Goddesses created the world: Din (Goddess of Power) forged the red earth, Nayru (Goddess of Wisdom) poured law and order into the land, and Farore (Goddess of Courage) created all living souls. When they departed for the heavens, they left behind the Triforce—three golden triangles resting in the Sacred Realm that grant the wish of whoever touches them, splitting into three parts if the user's heart is unbalanced.

The Three-Way Timeline Split

At the end of Ocarina of Time, Zelda uses the Ocarina to send Link back to his childhood, fracturing history into three separate branches: the Downfall Timeline (where Link is defeated by Ganon, leading to A Link to the Past and the original NES games), the Child Timeline (where Link warns the Royal Family of Ganondorf's plot early, leading to Majora's Mask and Twilight Princess), and the Adult Timeline (the world Link left behind, which floods and leads to The Wind Waker).

The Eternal Cycle of Reincarnation

Because of Demise's dying curse at the end of Skyward Sword, whenever great evil rises to threaten Hyrule, an incarnation of his hatred (such as Ganondorf) appears alongside a new hero bearing the spirit of the Hero and a princess carrying the blood of the Goddess Hylia.

Hidden Easter Eggs & Universe Secrets

  • The Hero's Shade: The skeletal warrior who teaches Link secret sword techniques in Twilight Princess is canonically the lingering spirit of the Hero of Time from Ocarina of Time and Majora's Mask, regretting that he was never remembered as a hero in the Child Timeline.
  • The Sheikah Eye Symbol: The iconic Sheikah crest—an eye with three triangles above and a single teardrop below—was originally designed without the tear. Lore suggests the teardrop was added after the Sheikah were betrayed or exiled by the Hyrulean Royal Family.
  • Fierce Deity's Origin: According to Zelda creators and manga lore, the Fierce Deity's Mask in Majora's Mask contains the dark, godlike memories and power of all the people of Termina, making it strong enough to rival Majora's evil.
  • Draconification: Revealed in Tears of the Kingdom, swallowing a Secret Stone turns any mortal into an immortal dragon at the cost of their memories and sense of self—suggesting the three elemental dragons (Dinraal, Naydra, and Farosh) may have once been ancient mortals or Zonai.
