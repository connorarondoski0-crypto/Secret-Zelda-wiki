Ocarina of Time (OoT)

Info and lore from Ocarina of Time (1998):

Link & Navi

Also known as the Hero of Time. Raised as a Kokiri child without a fairy until Navi is sent by the Great Deku Tree, he pulls the Master Sword from the Temple of Time to travel 7 years into the future to save Hyrule.

Zelda

Princess of Hyrule who foresees Ganon's evil plot in her dreams. As an adult during Ganon's reign, she aids Link while hiding in disguise as the Sheikah warrior Sheik, bearing the Triforce of Wisdom.

Ganondorf

The King of the Gerudo tribe who tricks Link into opening the Sacred Realm, claiming the Triforce of Power and transforming into the Demon King Ganon.

The Ocarina & Seven Sages

Using the royal Ocarina of Time, Link learns mystical melodies to warp across Hyrule, manipulate time, and awaken the Seven Sages who seal Ganon away in the Evil Realm.

Majora's Mask (MM)

Info and lore from Majora's Mask (2000):

Setting & The Three-Day Cycle

A direct sequel to Ocarina of Time set in the parallel world of Termina. Link has only 72 hours before a monstrous moon crashes into Clock Town, forcing him to repeatedly play the Song of Time to rewind the three-day cycle.

Skull Kid & Majora's Mask

A lonely Skull Kid steals a cursed, ancient hexing mask from the Happy Mask Salesman. The evil entity within Majora's Mask possesses Skull Kid, using his grief to pull the moon down from the sky.

Transformation Masks & The Four Giants

By healing troubled souls with the Song of Healing, Link obtains masks that transform him into a Deku Scrub, Goron, and Zora, allowing him to free the Four Giants who protect Termina.

The Wind Waker (TLOZWW)

Info and lore from The Wind Waker (2002):

Setting

Set hundreds of years after Ocarina of Time in a flooded Hyrule known as the Great Sea, where the Goddesses sealed ancient Hyrule beneath the ocean waves to stop Ganon's return.

Link & Tetra

Known as the Hero of Winds, this Link sets sail from Outset Island to rescue his sister Aryll and teams up with Tetra, a fiery pirate captain who is actually Princess Zelda's descendant.

The King of Red Lions

Link sails across the Great Sea aboard a talking red boat named the King of Red Lions, who is secretly Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule, the last king of ancient Hyrule.

Key Artifact

Link uses the Wind Waker, a magical conductor's baton, to control the direction of the wind and command ancient powers.

Twilight Princess (TLOZTP)

Info and lore from Twilight Princess (2006):

Setting & Transformation

Hyrule is engulfed by a parallel dimension called the Twilight Realm. When Link enters the Twilight, the Triforce of Courage protects him by transforming him into a divine beast—a wolf—rather than a lost spirit.

Midna

An impish creature from the Twilight Realm who rides on Wolf Link's back. She acts as Link's companion and is revealed to be the true ruler of the Twili, the Twilight Princess.

Zant, Ganondorf & The Mirror of Twilight

Usurper King Zant overthrows Midna and invades Hyrule using dark magic granted by Ganondorf, shattering the Mirror of Twilight to sever the link between worlds.

Skyward Sword (TLOZSS)

Info and lore from Skyward Sword (2011):

Timeline Origins

The earliest game in the official Zelda timeline, detailing the forging of the Master Sword, the Triforce legend, and the founding of the kingdom of Hyrule on the Surface below.

Skyloft & Loftwings

Humanity lives on floating islands above the clouds called Skyloft, navigating the heavens on giant guardian birds known as Loftwings.

Fi, Ghirahim & Demise

Link is guided by Fi, an analytical spirit residing within the Goddess Sword, while battling Demon Lord Ghirahim. At the climax, the Demon King Demise places an eternal curse binding his hatred to reincarnate against the blood of the Goddess and the spirit of the Hero forever.

Breath of the Wild (BOTW)

Info and lore from Breath of the Wild (2017):

The Great Calamity

100 years before the main story, Calamity Ganon corrupted Hyrule's ancient Sheikah technology (the Guardians and Divine Beasts), mortally wounding Link and devastating the kingdom.

The Slumber of Restoration

Link wakes up a century later inside the Shrine of Resurrection with no memories, guided by the Sheikah Slate to recover his past, reclaim the Master Sword, and assist Zelda, who has spent 100 years holding Ganon at bay inside Hyrule Castle.

The Four Champions

Mipha (Zora), Daruk (Goron), Revali (Rito), and Urbosa (Gerudo) were chosen warriors who piloted the Divine Beasts. Though they fell during the Calamity, their spirits grant Link powerful abilities to defeat Ganon.
