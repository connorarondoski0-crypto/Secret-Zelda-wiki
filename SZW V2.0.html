Ocarina of Time (OoT)

Info and lore from Ocarina of Time (1998):

Link

Also known as the Hero of Time. Raised as a Kokiri child, he pulls the Master Sword from the Temple of Time to travel 7 years into the future to save Hyrule.

Zelda

Princess of Hyrule who foresees Ganon's evil plot in her dreams. As an adult during Ganon's reign, she aids Link while hiding in disguise as the Sheikah warrior Sheik.

Ganondorf

The King of the Gerudo tribe who tricks Link into opening the Sacred Realm, claiming the Triforce of Power and transforming into the Demon King Ganon.

The Wind Waker (TLOZWW)

Info and lore from The Wind Waker (2002):

Setting

Set hundreds of years after Ocarina of Time in a flooded Hyrule known as the Great Sea, where ancient Hyrule lies forgotten beneath the ocean waves.

Link & Tetra

Known as the Hero of Winds, this Link sets sail from Outset Island to rescue his sister Aryll and teams up with Tetra, a fiery pirate captain who is actually Princess Zelda's descendant.

Key Artifact

Link uses the Wind Waker, a magical conductor's baton, to control the direction of the wind and command ancient powers.

Twilight Princess (TLOZTP)

Info and lore from Twilight Princess (2006):

Setting & Transformation

Hyrule is engulfed by a parallel dimension called the Twilight Realm. When Link enters the Twilight, he transforms into a wolf rather than a spirit.

Midna

An impish creature from the Twilight Realm who rides on Wolf Link's back. She acts as Link's companion and is revealed to be the true Twilight Princess.

Zant & Ganondorf

Usurper King Zant overthrows Midna and invades Hyrule, backed by the lingering dark power of Ganondorf.

Skyward Sword (TLOZSS)

Info and lore from Skyward Sword (2011):

Timeline Origins

The earliest game in the official Zelda timeline, detailing the origins of the Master Sword, the Triforce legend, and the eternal curse connecting Link, Zelda, and evil.

Skyloft & Loftwings

Humanity lives on floating islands in the sky called Skyloft, navigating the heavens on giant magical birds known as Loftwings.

Fi & Demise

Link is guided by Fi, a spirit residing within the Goddess Sword. He battles the Demon King Demise, whose final curse binds his hatred to reincarnate against the blood of the Goddess and the spirit of the Hero forever.

Breath of the Wild (BOTW)

Info and lore from Breath of the Wild (2017):

The Calamity

100 years before the main story, Calamity Ganon corrupted Hyrule's ancient Sheikah technology (the Guardians and Divine Beasts), defeating Link and devastating the kingdom.

The Slumber of Restoration

Link wakes up a century later with no memories, guided by the Sheikah Slate to free the four Divine Beasts and assist Zelda, who has spent 100 years holding Ganon at bay inside Hyrule Castle.

The Four Champions

Mipha (Zora), Daruk (Goron), Revali (Rito), and Urbosa (Gerudo) were chosen warriors who piloted the Divine Beasts to aid Link and Zelda in fighting the Calamity.
